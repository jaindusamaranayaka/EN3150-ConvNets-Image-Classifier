    \subsubsection{Momentum interpretation}\label{momentum-interpretation}

The SGD with Momentum run uses a momentum coefficient of 0.9. Momentum
accumulates a decaying history of gradients, which can speed updates
along directions that consistently reduce the loss and damp oscillation
across steep directions. It can also overshoot minima when the learning
rate and momentum are too large. Compare the measured validation curves,
accuracy, and loss for SGD and SGD with Momentum after the three runs
finish; this notebook does not yet contain completed measurements.
